SALESIQ ANNUAL SALES REPORT & STRATEGY DOCUMENT — FY 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
This report covers the full fiscal year 2024 performance for SalesIQ Technologies Pvt. Ltd., a B2B office equipment and electronics distributor operating across four regions in India: North, South, East, and West. Total company revenue for FY2024 exceeded expectations, driven primarily by strong Electronics category performance and exceptional effort from the North region team.

The company set an ambitious annual revenue target of ₹90,00,000 at the beginning of FY2024. Based on preliminary figures, the company is projected to exceed this target by approximately 12%, which management attributes to the successful onboarding of five new enterprise accounts and an expanded product catalogue including the Laptop Pro and Monitor 27inch lines.

REGIONAL STRATEGY
-----------------
North Region: The North has historically been our strongest region and continued that trend in FY2024. Key accounts include TechCorp Ltd and EnterpriseCo. The strategy here focuses on high-value electronics sales and long-term contract renewals. Target for FY2025: grow revenue by 18% through deeper penetration of the enterprise segment.

South Region: The South region showed steady growth, particularly in the Furniture category. The region benefits from a growing startup ecosystem in Bengaluru and Chennai. GreenOffice and BizSpace Inc remain anchor clients. Strategy: expand SMB outreach and introduce bundled product offers.

East Region: The East is an emerging market for us. Rohit Verma's consistent performance has anchored this region. DataSoft Pvt and LogiPark are key accounts. The focus for FY2025 is to increase accessories sales and cross-sell electronics.

West Region: The West region has the most diverse client base. Sneha Patel is one of the top performers company-wide. FinTech Solutions and StartupHub drive significant volume. The region has shown the fastest growth rate in accessories, particularly wireless peripherals.

PRODUCT STRATEGY
----------------
Laptop Pro remains our flagship and highest-revenue product. Despite being the most expensive item in our catalogue at ₹75,000 per unit, it consistently generates the highest total revenue and attracts enterprise-level clients. Margin optimization efforts are planned for FY2025.

Monitor 27inch has emerged as a strong secondary product, often sold alongside Laptops. Bundle pricing will be explored in Q1 FY2025.

Accessories (Wireless Mouse, Keyboard Mech) represent the highest volume by units. Gross margins on accessories are thinner but the volume helps with overall throughput and introduces new customers to the portfolio.

Furniture (Office Chair, Standing Desk) provides stable, predictable revenue. Demand has increased with the post-pandemic return-to-office trend. We plan to introduce 3 new ergonomic furniture products in FY2025.

SALES TEAM PERFORMANCE NOTES
-----------------------------
Amit Sharma (North) has been the highest performer for the second consecutive year. His ability to manage large enterprise accounts while maintaining strong client relationships sets a benchmark for the team. He is being considered for a Senior Sales Manager role in FY2025.

Priya Rao (South) has shown exceptional improvement in Q3 and Q4. Her strength lies in repeat business and high customer satisfaction scores. She received the "Most Improved" internal award for FY2024.

Sneha Patel (West) is the most versatile rep on the team, handling both high-value electronics and bulk accessories orders. Her pipeline management skills are exemplary.

Rohit Verma (East) consistently meets targets and has built strong trust with clients in a relatively newer market. Focused coaching on enterprise sales techniques is planned for him in FY2025.

Karan Mehta (North/coverage) joined mid-year and performed above expectations for a new hire. He shows particular strength in the Furniture segment.

KEY RISKS & CHALLENGES
-----------------------
1. Supply chain disruptions for imported electronics components may affect Laptop Pro availability in Q1 FY2025.
2. Increasing competition from e-commerce platforms (Amazon Business, Flipkart B2B) puts pressure on accessories pricing.
3. Client concentration risk: TechCorp Ltd accounts for a significant portion of North region revenue. Diversification is a priority.
4. Talent retention: Two regional sales positions remain unfilled. Recruitment is a top priority for Q1 FY2025.

FY2025 TARGETS & GOALS
------------------------
- Overall revenue target: ₹1,10,00,000 (approximately 22% growth)
- Expand into 2 new cities: Pune and Ahmedabad
- Launch a customer loyalty programme by Q2 FY2025
- Achieve average profit margin of 30% across all products
- Onboard minimum 10 new enterprise accounts
- Launch an online ordering portal for repeat clients by Q3 FY2025

CONCLUSION
----------
FY2024 has been a strong year of growth and team development for SalesIQ Technologies. The combination of a high-performing team, an expanding product catalogue, and improving regional strategies has positioned the company well for its FY2025 ambitions. The management team remains committed to sustainable growth, client satisfaction, and developing the sales team into a world-class operation.
